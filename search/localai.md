# LocalAI

[← Back to search](README.md)

**Role:** candidate inference engine. **License:** MIT.

## Benefits
- OpenAI-compatible server with 60+ backends, including llama.cpp and vLLM; backends are pulled on demand.
- No GPU required; CUDA, ROCm, oneAPI, Metal and Vulkan acceleration available; container install.
- Could act as a single front for both [Ollama](ollama.md)-style and [vLLM](vllm.md)-style serving.

## Weaknesses / must-know
- One more layer to learn and package.
